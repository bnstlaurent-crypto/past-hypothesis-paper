Verification scripts. Run from this folder:
  python3 v_part1.py   (analytic lemmas, constants of Lemmas A1-A4)
  python3 v_part2.py   (theorem constants, thresholds, MPS dimension)
  python3 v_part3.py   (chaos check, Tables 1-2 from raw data; needs the raw .jsonl files unpacked here)
  python3 v_part4.py   (robust-lemma identity, ETH-A inequalities)
verification_log.txt holds the outputs. dbg.py documents the two initial test-code failures.
